Hello, I'm David. I work in information technology leadership in the Portland
Metro area. I prefer open source platforms and tools, like Linux and PHP, but I
get along with most anything. I also spent nearly 20 years teaching information
technology classes part-time at Mt. Hood Community College, both in the evenings
and online. If you're curious about my professional background, my
[LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/david-m-cramblett) has the details.

I enjoy a large variety of outdoor adventures, but my real passion lies with
Alpinism. I live in the heart of the Cascade range and enjoy climbs on all of
the local volcanoes, ice climbing in Bozeman MT, Cody WY, and Ouray CO, and
exploring the North Cascades and the big ice of nearby Canada.
